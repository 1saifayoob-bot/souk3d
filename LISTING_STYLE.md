# Souk3D Listing Style Guide

This is the template every Souk3D product listing follows. The AI listing generator (`api/generate-listing.js`, constant `SOUK3D_STYLE`) is built from this file. Update both together.

## The voice

Modern, Arab, nostalgic, playful, design-conscious. Recognizable. Cultural. Warm. A little witty. Never cheesy. Never over-written.

Souk3D turns familiar Arab memories, sayings, objects and everyday culture into things you can hold, display, gift and keep. An Arab customer should think "I know exactly what this is", and someone unfamiliar with the reference should still understand the product.

Not overly traditional. Not fake luxury. Not Amazon keyword stuffing. Not AI-poetic. Not a museum label.

## The biggest rule: don't overwrite

Hook + description together: **40–80 words**. If a sentence could describe 5,000 other Etsy products, delete it.

## Listing template

| Part | Rule |
|---|---|
| **Title (EN)** | Clear searchable name first, personality second. Max ~60 characters. |
| **Title (AR)** | How an Arabic speaker would naturally say it, not a machine translation. |
| **Hook** | One human sentence: what it is and why it's special, or which memory it represents. |
| **Description** | 2–4 short sentences: cultural connection → what it is → where/how you'd use it → optional gift line. |
| **Details** | Scannable facts, one per line: what's included, material, size, how it's made, color variation, care. |
| **Description (AR)** | Localized, not translated. Same feeling, can use different words. |
| **Keywords** | SEO terms live here, never stuffed into the title or description. |

## Pick the lead by product type

- **Cultural / nostalgic:** lead with the memory. *If you grew up seeing these cups around the house, you already know.*
- **Funny:** lead with the joke. *For the person who says "yalla" and then takes another 20 minutes to leave.*
- **Arabic typography:** lead with the meaning. *حبيبي — one little word with a lot behind it.*
- **City / country:** lead with identity and place. *A little piece of Damascus, wherever home is today.*
- **Functional:** lead with what it does. *Your everyday cable problem, cleaned up in one tiny piece.*

Recognition rule: if almost every Arab customer will recognize the reference, don't explain it. If it's regional, historical or niche, add one sentence of context at most.

## Banned openers and filler

Elevate your space · Add a touch of · Beautifully crafted · Meticulously designed · Perfect blend of · Timeless elegance · Whether you're looking for · Transform your everyday space · attention to detail · perfect addition · look no further · must-have

The generator checks for these automatically and sends a draft back for one rewrite if it breaks the rules.

## Be precise about "handmade"

- Never call PLA "ceramic". Never say "hand-painted" if the color comes from printing.
- Only use "3D printed and finished by hand", "made in small batches", "designed and 3D printed in our studio" or "made in the USA" when it's true for that product.
- Give the facts in the admin "Facts for the AI" box (e.g. *set of 2, approx 3 in each, magnetic back, 3D printed PLA*). The AI never invents sizes, counts or materials.

## Arabic voice

Easy, modern Arabic with an occasional conversational touch. Never stiff corporate Arabic.

- ❌ أضف لمسة فنية ساحرة على مساحتك باستخدام مجموعة مغناطيس…
- ✅ قطعة صغيرة من ذكريات القهوة العربية ☕ مستوحاة من فناجين القهوة الملوّنة اللي نعرفها ونحبها. حطّها على الثلاجة، في ركن القهوة، أو اهديها لشخص رح يعرف الحكاية من أول نظرة.

## Example

**Title:** Arabic Coffee Cup Magnet Set
**Arabic title:** طقم مغناطيس فناجين قهوة عربية
**Hook:** You know the cup. You know the coffee. And you probably know exactly whose kitchen this reminds you of.
**Description:** Inspired by the colorful cups that have lived in Arab kitchens for generations, this magnet set brings a familiar piece of coffee culture to your fridge. Keep it for yourself or gift it to someone who'll get the reference immediately.
**Details:** Set of 2 · 3D printed · Approx. 3" each · Magnetic backing · Colors may vary slightly between pieces

## The two tests before publishing

1. Could Amazon, Temu, Etsy or another gift store put its logo above this exact description? If yes, rewrite it.
2. Can I understand what I'm buying in 10 seconds? If not, simplify.
